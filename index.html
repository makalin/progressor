              About PROGRESSOR

              A high-performance harmonic engine for musicians who treat improvisation as a science. Uses a weighted graph (circle of fifths, modal interchange) to suggest smooth chord transitions and voice-leading. Build progressions, see Roman numerals, borrow chords from parallel minor, and export to JSON or Rust.
